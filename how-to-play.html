Game Rules

How To Play

Kultura plays like classic Monopoly — with one powerful twist. Master the board, collect properties, and unleash your Character Card to dominate Nusantara.

🏆
Objective

How To Win

👑
Be the last player remaining with money

The goal is to be the wealthiest player. Buy properties, charge rent, and bankrupt your opponents. The last player who has not gone bankrupt wins the game.

💸
Bankruptcy

A player is bankrupt when they owe more money than they can raise by mortgaging or selling all their properties. They must hand everything to their creditor and leave the game.

🏘️
Monopoly

Own all properties of the same colour group to form a Monopoly. Only then can you start building houses and hotels to dramatically increase rent.

⏱️
Time Limit Variant

Agree on a time limit before starting. When time is up, count each player's total assets — cash plus property values. The wealthiest player wins.

🗺️
Overview

What The Game Is About

Each player starts with the same amount of money. You take turns rolling dice and moving around the board. When you land on an unowned property you may buy it — otherwise it goes to auction.

When an opponent lands on your property, they pay you rent. The rent rises sharply when you own all properties in a colour group and begin adding houses and hotels.

Community Chest and Chance cards can reward or punish you. Taxes, fines, and the dreaded Go to Jail space keep every trip around the board unpredictable.

Mix in Kultura Character Cards and the strategic depth increases — a single legendary card can flip the fortune of any round.

Before You Start

Set Up

1
Place the board & sort the money

Unfold the Kultura board in the centre of the table. Sort all the Monopoly money into denominations and place it beside the board to form the Bank. Choose one player to be the Banker — they manage the Bank's money, Title Deeds, and buildings.

2
Each player takes their starting money

The Banker gives every player their starting amount of $1,500 divided as:

2 × $500
2 × $100
2 × $50
6 × $20
5 × $10
5 × $5
5 × $1
3
Choose your token & place it on GO

Each player selects a token — in Kultura these are cultural symbols of Nusantara. Place all tokens on the GO space. The player who rolls the highest number goes first; play continues clockwise.

4
Shuffle the cards & place them face-down

Shuffle the Chance cards and place them face-down on their board space. Do the same for Community Chest cards. Place all Title Deed cards beside the board, sorted by colour group — the Banker will hand them out when properties are purchased.

★
Deal each player one Kultura Character Card

Before the game begins, each player draws 1 Kultura Character Card from the shuffled Karakter deck. Keep it face-down — this is your secret boost. You may play it once at any point during the game on your own turn. See the Character Card Rule below for full details.

🎲
Gameplay

On Your Turn

Step 1

🎲 Roll the dice & move

Roll both dice and move your token that many spaces forward (always clockwise). If you roll doubles, take another turn after resolving the current one. Roll doubles three times in a row and you go directly to Jail.

💡 Each time you pass or land on GO, collect $200 salary from the Bank.
Step 2

🏘️ Buy, pay rent, or follow instructions

Depending on where you land, one of the following happens:

🏷️
Unowned Property

You may buy it from the Bank at the printed price and receive its Title Deed. If you choose not to buy, the Banker auctions it to the highest bidder — any player may bid, including you.

💰
Owned Property

Pay rent to the owner as shown on the Title Deed. Rent doubles if the owner holds all properties of that colour group. Houses and hotels raise rent dramatically.

🃏
Chance / Community Chest

Draw the top card, follow its instructions — collect money, pay fines, advance to a space, or go directly to Jail. Return the card face-down to the bottom of the pile.

🧾
Tax Spaces

Income Tax: pay $200 or 10% of your total assets — your choice. Luxury Tax: pay $100 to the Bank. There are no exemptions.

🚔
Go To Jail

Move directly to Jail without collecting $200. To get out: roll doubles on one of your next three turns, pay a $50 fine before rolling, or use a Get Out of Jail Free card.

🅿️
Free Parking

Nothing happens. Rest and plan your next move. (The popular house rule of collecting fines here is not part of the official rules.)

Step 3

🏗️ Build houses & hotels

Once you own every property in a colour group (a Monopoly), you may buy houses from the Bank. Place them evenly across your properties — you cannot put a second house on one property until all others in that group also have one. Four houses can be upgraded to a hotel, removing the houses and returning them to the Bank.

Step 4

🤝 Trade & mortgage

At any time during your turn, you may trade properties, cash, and Get Out of Jail Free cards with other players — any deal both parties agree to is legal. If you need cash urgently, mortgage a property to the Bank for half its printed price. No rent is collected on a mortgaged property. Pay 110% of the mortgage value to lift it.

★
Kultura Special Rule

The Character Card Rule

✦ ADDITIONAL RULE ✦

Each player may play their Character Card ONCE per game

This is the only rule added on top of standard Monopoly. Everything else follows classic rules exactly.

🃏
You hold 1 Character Card

At the start of the game, each player draws one Kultura Character Card from the Karakter deck and keeps it face-down in front of them. Do not reveal it to other players until you play it.

⏰
Play it on your turn — any time, once

On any of your turns, before or after rolling the dice, you may reveal and activate your Character Card. Announce its effect out loud, apply it immediately, then place the card face-up in a discard pile. You cannot play it again.

⚡
The card effect applies for that turn only

Unless the card explicitly says otherwise, its effect lasts only for the turn you play it. Some cards modify your dice roll, some affect rent you pay or collect, and some trigger a one-time action. Read the card text carefully.

🚫
You cannot trade or discard your card

Character Cards are bound to the player who drew them. They cannot be traded, sold, gifted, or discarded. If you go bankrupt without playing your card, it goes into the discard pile unused.

Reference

Board Spaces

🏁
GO

Collect $200 every time you pass or land on GO.

🏘️
Properties

Buy, own, and charge rent. Build houses & hotels once you hold the full colour group.

🚂
Stations

Rent is $25, $50, $100, or $200 depending on how many stations the owner holds.

💡
Utilities

Own one: rent = 4× dice roll. Own both: rent = 10× dice roll.

🃏
Chance

Draw a card and follow its instructions — could be money, movement, or Jail.

📋
Community Chest

Draw a card — usually involves paying or collecting money from the Bank or other players.

🧾
Income Tax

Pay $200 or 10% of total assets — you decide which before counting.

💎
Luxury Tax

Pay $100 to the Bank. No exceptions.

🚔
Jail / Just Visiting

Just visiting — no effect. Sent here by Go To Jail or card: miss up to 3 turns unless you roll doubles, pay $50, or use a card.

🅿️
Free Parking

Safe haven — nothing happens when you land here.

🚧
Go To Jail

Move directly to Jail. Do not pass GO. Do not collect $200.

🎴
Kultura Draw

Kultura-exclusive space: draw a random Kultura card to keep in your hand until played.

Card Reference

Kultura Card Types

All four card types are part of the Kultura collection. However only Karakter cards are used during gameplay as the Character Card Boost. The other three types are collectibles.

👤
Karakter
⚡ Used in gameplay

Historical figures and legends of Indonesia. Each Karakter card has a unique ability that boosts your turn when played. This is the only card type that can be activated during a game.

Ki Hajar Dewantara · Sultan HB · R.A. Kartini
🍛
Makanan
🏅 Collectible

Regional cuisines of the Nusantara archipelago. Collectible cards celebrating Indonesia's rich culinary heritage — from Gudeg to Bakpia. Not used in board gameplay.

Gudeg · Bakpia Pathok · Soto Ayam
🏛️
Bangunan
🏅 Collectible

Iconic cultural sites and traditional architecture across Indonesia. Collectible cards showcasing Nusantara's architectural wonders. Not used in board gameplay.

Keraton · Borobudur · Prambanan
👘
Pakaian
🏅 Collectible

Traditional garments and textiles from across the archipelago. Collectible cards honouring Indonesia's textile arts and ceremonial dress. Not used in board gameplay.

Kebaya Jawa · Batik Parang · Blangkon
